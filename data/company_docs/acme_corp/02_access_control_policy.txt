# Acme Corp Access Control Policy

## Document Information

Policy Owner: Information Security Department  
Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved  
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines requirements for controlling access to Acme Corp information systems and information assets.

## 2. Access Authorization

Access to company systems shall be granted only to authorized users based on business requirements.

All new user access requests must be approved by the user's manager before access is provisioned.

## 3. Least Privilege

Users shall receive only the minimum level of access required to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Administrative privileges shall be restricted to authorized personnel.

## 4. User Account Management

Each user shall be assigned an individual account.

Shared user accounts should not be used unless there is a documented business or technical requirement.

## 5. Authentication

Users must authenticate before accessing protected company systems.

Multi-factor authentication shall be required for remote administrative access.

## 6. Termination and Role Changes

User access shall be removed when employment is terminated.

Managers are responsible for requesting changes to access when an employee changes roles.

## 7. Privileged Access

Privileged accounts shall only be provided to personnel who require administrative capabilities for their job responsibilities.

Privileged accounts shall not be used for normal daily activities.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be documented and approved by the Information Security Manager.
