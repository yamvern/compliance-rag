# Acme Corp Information Security Policy

## Document Information

Policy Owner: Information Security Department  
Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved  
Effective Date: 2026-01-01  
Review Frequency: Annual

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish the information security principles and responsibilities used by Acme Corp to protect its information systems, business information, and technology assets.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, information systems, applications, devices, and information assets owned or managed by Acme Corp.

## 3. Information Security Governance

Acme Corp shall maintain an information security program aligned with organizational objectives and cybersecurity risk.

Management is responsible for supporting the information security program and ensuring that appropriate security responsibilities are assigned.

The Information Security Manager is responsible for coordinating information security activities and reporting significant cybersecurity risks to management.

## 4. Risk Management

Cybersecurity risks shall be identified, documented, assessed, and reviewed.

Risk assessments shall consider threats, vulnerabilities, business impact, and existing security controls.

Significant cybersecurity risks shall be communicated to appropriate management personnel.

## 5. Access Control

Access to Acme Corp information systems shall be limited to authorized users.

Access shall be granted according to business requirements and the principle of least privilege.

User access must be approved before access to production information systems is provided.

## 6. Asset Protection

Information and technology assets shall be identified and protected according to their importance to the organization.

Employees are responsible for protecting company devices and information assigned to them.

## 7. Security Awareness

Employees shall receive information security awareness training.

Employees are responsible for reporting suspected cybersecurity incidents and protecting authentication credentials.

## 8. Incident Management

Suspected information security incidents shall be reported to the Information Security Department.

The organization shall maintain procedures for responding to cybersecurity incidents.

## 9. Business Continuity

Acme Corp shall maintain appropriate backup and recovery capabilities for important information systems and business information.

## 10. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and when significant changes occur to the organization, technology environment, or cybersecurity risk profile.
